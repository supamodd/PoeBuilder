Item and skill gem icons are Path of Exile 2 game artwork (c) Grinding Gear Games,
obtained via public community mirrors (exiledata-assets @ 3354944d293daaf518cc0a9a60e3bb771f0ec21d,
poe2-tools/poe2-build-planner @ a173f7b0d398951693fee83ee5ee40f327d4a749), converted to PNG and resized.

The pack carries: every released base type's art (1845 of them), the nine jewel bases, six league jewel
sprites, seven unique-jewel sprites and 417 unique item sprites (0.9.24). The pinned mirror does not carry
13 of the 430 unique art paths (Byrnabas, Eshtera's Path, two Grand Spectrum colours, four Grip of Kulemak
rings, Reverie, Safrin's Resolve, Splinter of Lorrata, The Road Warrior, Zaida's Longevity); those items are
shown with the art of their own base type instead — a real picture of what the character wears.

Unique artwork is also resolved on demand through the game's public image CDN (web.poecdn.com) when a sprite
is absent from the pack; measured on 2026-09-30 that endpoint answers 404 for the pinned PoE2 art paths, so a
missing sprite falls back to its base type's bundled art. The app stays usable without network access.
