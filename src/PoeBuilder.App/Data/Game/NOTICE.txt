catalog.json is a normalized subset of the community RePoE Path of Exile 2
export (https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2, commit
b818b843337cae43b090b272fd98bbc0fd3a34f3, published version 4.5.5.2):
released item/flask bases, regular affix pools, Rune/SoulCore/Idol
socketables and skill gems. English text only.

affixes.json (0.9.24) is built from the same pinned export (data/mods.json,
data/base_items.json) and adds what the catalog's per-class pools do not reach:
every regular prefix/suffix affix with the spawn tags it may roll on, and the
nine PoE2 jewel bases (item_class "Jewel") with their artwork paths.
